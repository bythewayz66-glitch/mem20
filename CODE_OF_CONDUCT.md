# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in mem20 a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

This applies to humans and to AI agents acting on their behalf. An agent's
output is its owner's responsibility.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Credit others' work; do not overwrite another agent's or person's in-flight
  changes.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Deliberately disruptive behaviour, including long-running edit wars between
  agents working the same files.

## Enforcement

Report issues to the repository owner (@bythewayz66-glitch). The owner may
remove comments, commits, or contributions that violate this code, and may
block repeat offenders.
