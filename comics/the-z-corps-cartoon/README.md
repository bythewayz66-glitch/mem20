# Z CORPS — cartoon

The cartoon series based on THE Z CORPS comic. Same world, but the camera pulls
out of the server cathedral and follows the characters home.

Each cartoon episode matches a comic book issue, with added comedy, action,
suspense, and plot twists. The comics are chopped into little bursts of story
and they keep narrowing to mem20 — which is what the comics are supposed to do.
The cartoon fixes that: it has a proper storyline that survives multiple
seasons, built on the characters' lives **outside** mem20.

## The show

- **Main character:** Jayson. His friend Gabe hangs out every episode.
  Every episode they snort green space dust and smoke from a random bong Gabe
  built out of random items — a new bong every episode.
- **Jme and Jayson** are married and they love each other. Jayson and Jammee
  usually both hang out at Gabe's water shack, but not always both.
- **The other main side characters** (O-pen-code, Claud's Curser, H3RM3S /
  Hermes, Kimi Moonshot, Grok, and the rest) are AI easter eggs — personified
  nods to real AI tools and models. Throughout the series, all the LLMs
  randomly show up at Gabe's water shack to buy space dust.
- **Muse** is the one who remembers — the memory of the water shack — and her
  pet **Playto** is a robotic dachshund.
- **The last scene of every episode** is Jayson and Jammee ending their
  night, telling each other "I love you," and the screen fades to black.
  The series finale is the one exception: it ends on the shack at dawn, with
  all of the bongs on the counter.

## The storyline

The through-line is the 23rd slot: the green set has twenty-two packages, the
twenty-third was deleted four days before anybody wrote the rule, and somebody
is standing in it. That somebody is Jayson himself, 500 years from now.

O-pen-code and Claud's Curser are alternate-reality versions of mem20.
Claud is trying to destroy reality by kidnapping O-pen-code's agents — Big
Pickle, Iceman, and Spacebunny. His assistants are Beam and Longcat. Hermes
is a criminal for hire and a jack of all trades.

- **Season 1 — "The Count"** (14 episodes, one per comic issue): Jayson tries
  to get a true count of his own company. Finale: he sees the figure in the
  23rd slot turn around — and it is himself, 500 years from now.
- **Season 2 — "The Hole"**: the person standing in the 23rd slot, and
  Claud's plan to destroy reality.
- **Season 3 — "The Replacement"**: Beam inside the private team, and Jayson
  choosing between the count and his life outside it.

Palette: obsidian `#0C0C0C`, charcoal, bone `#DED6C4`, acid green `#CCFF00`.
Scratchy dry-brush inking, dense cross-hatching, heavy spotted blacks.
Newsprint grain, distressed edges, aged paper. MEM20 COMICS masthead, issue
number in ochre, code-authority footer.
