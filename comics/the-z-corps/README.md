# THE Z CORPS — comics

The complete comic series built from the mem20 chronicle. Every issue is a fully
illustrated PDF in the locked series visual identity — obsidian/charcoal/bone with
acid-green `#CCFF00` accents, scratchy dry-brush inking, cross-hatching, newsprint
grain and distressed trade dress — followed by its character bible cards.

Each issue ships with a shooting script carrying the page-by-page breakdown and the
running-gags sheet.

## Source

Every issue is drawn from the published chronicle at
[mem20-naration](https://github.com/bythewayz66-glitch/mem20) — the commit record,
the failures corpus, the diary files and the deployment ledger. Nothing in the
comics is invented lore: where the record is silent, the caption says so, and where
two figures disagree the issue prints both and names the source that settled it.

## Visual identity (locked)

- Palette: obsidian `#0C0C0C`, charcoal, bone `#DED6C4`, acid green `#CCFF00`
- Inking: scratchy dry brush, dense cross-hatching, heavy spotted blacks
- Texture: newsprint grain, distressed edges, aged paper
- Trade dress: MEM20 COMICS masthead, issue number in ochre, code-authority footer
- Character cards are the source of truth; pages are drawn from the cards, never the reverse
