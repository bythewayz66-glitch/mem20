# THE Z CORPS #1 — "CHAT DOES NOT COUNT"
## Illustrated Shooting Script (34 pages) — dialogue pass

**Story & Art Direction:** Grok + JMoore
**Tone:** 7.8 — gritty noir, precise, darkly satirical
**Narrator:** Scribe

**Permanent Art Note:** In every Z Corps interior, a wall-mounted Tools / Domains counter is visible and is always wrong.

**Humor sourcing rule:** every joke in this issue is a real mem20 incident, quoted or lightly dramatised. Nothing invented. The numbers are the numbers that were measured.

---

## THE RUNNING GAGS (established here, reusable in every future issue)

1. **The counter.** TOOLS 412 / DOMAINS 97. Always wrong. Nobody has ever seen it right. Somebody scratched IT IS NOT 412 into the housing with a screwdriver.
2. **The number that went down.** The expected tool count was edited six times in five minutes thirty-five seconds: 272, 290, **281**, 294, 298, 302, 306. The 281 is lower than the 290. Scribe will not let this go.
3. **Eighty-four identical programs.** 145 files named `runtime.py`. Fifteen distinct. Eighty-four are the same file. The Corps refused to merge them, and wrote the refusal down.
4. **The green that means red.** A test step that runs zero tests, exits 5, and passes. "The check is green because we told it to be green when it fails."
5. **/ready.** The only endpoint with a clean record. Polled by nothing. Scribe is fond of it.
6. **The ledger that went quiet.** Silent from 29 September. Then eight entries in one day, better written than the chronicle.
7. **The decimator that refused.** It would not return a broken mesh. Scribe considers this the most admirable thing in the estate.
8. **hyperfine.** A tool the chronicle was commissioned to describe. It does not exist. Not in any blob, any branch, any commit message.
