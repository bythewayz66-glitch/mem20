**Structural spine:** Post 24 *The Gate That Did Not Move* (Week Twelve, 7 Nov 2026, 12,158 words), with the six live threads it names, the corrections log entries dated 7 November 2026, and the standing-facts list from Chapter Nine.

**Premise.** The twenty-third slot is a hole with a rule over it. The rule says *existing functionality is never removed, deleted, quarantined or dropped* — and the hole is where the twenty-third package used to be, deleted four days before anybody wrote the rule. This issue is about the difference between a rule and a practice, and about what happens when somebody finally falls in.

**Sourcing rule.** Every gag, number and quotation in this issue is a real mem20 incident from the chronicles, the audit corpus, the failures ledger or the diary. Nothing is invented. Where a figure is disputed the issue prints both figures and says which one the audit settled.

## THE LOCKED STYLE — BINDING ON EVERY PAGE

**PALETTE** — obsidian, charcoal and bone, with a single acid-green accent at `#CCFF00`. No second accent colour. Green is reserved for the one thing on a page that is *true and load-bearing*: a correct number, a working gate, a rule that is being read. It is never used for decoration.

**INKING** — scratchy dry-brush line, heavy cross-hatching, spotted blacks. Line weight carries hierarchy: 3px for panel borders, 2px for figure contours, 1px for hatching. No clean vector edges anywhere.

**TEXTURE** — newsprint grain over every page, 9–12% noise, plus a subtle paper-fibre mottle. Halftone dots only in flashback panels.

**TRADE DRESS** — MEM20 COMICS logo top-left, issue number, price, barcode bottom-right, thin light-blue signature `JMoore`. Distressed edges on the cover only.

**LETTERING** — captions in a monospaced typewriter face, dialogue in a hand-lettered comic face, sound effects in dry-brush display. Captions are always the narrator's voice: dry, deadpan, present tense, no exclamation marks.

**ON-MODEL NOTES (binding)** — Jayson: hoodie, unshaven from Issue #4 onward, the shave did not take. Scribe: coat, notebook, never without the notebook. The counter: dark from Issue #4 page 27 onward, a landmark, not a character. Drift: taller each issue, never touching the ceiling, and from Issue #5 it *looks at things*. The twenty-third slot: papered over with the rule, and the paper is legible in every panel it appears in.

**VOICE RULES** — six, unchanged from Issue #4:
1. Captions are dry and factual. They never explain a joke.
2. Scribe states the finding. Jayson states the objection. Neither wins.
3. Every number in dialogue is a real number from the record.
4. A gag pays off by being *stated plainly*, never by being underlined.
5. Private-life scenes have one location, two people, no plot, and one line that lands.
6. Nothing is invented. Where two figures disagree, both are printed and the settling source is named.
