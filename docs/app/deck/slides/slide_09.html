08 / WHAT 0.000 MEANS
We put our weakest number on its own slide
A board that finds a 0.000 hit rate in an appendix after the meeting stops trusting the other twelve slides. So: here is the row, here is what it costs us, and here is why it is not the headline.
WHAT IT SAYS
On adversarial queries, all three retrieval modes score zero.
Adversarial means the query is built to look answerable from a staler or adjacent record. n=3 per mode. A hit rate of 0.000 on n=3 is 3 misses — a real failure, and also a small denominator. Both facts belong on the slide.
WHY IT IS NOT A LEAK
Leak rate is 0.000 on every row.
The system does not answer from the wrong record. It declines to retrieve, and the call reports a diagnostic. Failing closed is a different — and far cheaper — failure than leaking a stale fact into an answer.

AND WE DO NOT COMPARE OURSELVES TO 92.5 / 94.4
Mem0's LoCoMo 92.5, LongMemEval 94.4 and Zep's DMR 94.8 come from different corpora — two of three private or vendor-defined — measured with different metrics. Nothing in our results reproduces or refutes them. The benchmark file says this in its own preamble; we are not going to quietly drop that caveat in a board deck.
