> **What broke.** The launch page stated no tool count at all, in a project whose positioning is that
> its numbers are measured. The retired 257/26 band lived in the surrounding documents, and the page
> deferred to `README.md` rather than carrying the figure.
>
> **What we learned.** An absence is harder to find than an error. A page that says 270 when the
> server serves 487 is wrong and can be grepped for. A page that says nothing is not wrong, and no
> scan for a retired band will find it — which is why the fix had to add the number rather than
> correct it. **The doc-count drift gate catches stale numbers; it cannot catch missing ones.** The
> gate's scope is "no retired band in any live doc," and a doc with no band passes it. That is a real
> limit of the control, and the launch-page record is the only place in the window where it is
> stated.
