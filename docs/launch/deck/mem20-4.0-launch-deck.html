01 · THE PROBLEM

An agent that remembers everything
it invents.

01 · COMPOUNDING

A guess becomes a fact

One hallucinated detail written to memory is retrieved as evidence on the next turn. By the tenth turn it is load-bearing.

02 · NO PROVENANCE

Nothing to audit

If a store cannot say where a fact came from or how confident it was, no post-hoc review can separate recall from invention.

03 · PROMPT RULES

A request, not a boundary

“Don’t make things up” in a system prompt is a convention. It is not enforced, not recorded, and not checkable after the fact.

The failure is not that the model is wrong. It is that the store cannot tell you it was wrong.
02 · THE WEDGE

Grounded and simulated never mix.

Two partitions in one store, with a hard boundary between them. Promotion is an explicit, recorded event — never a side effect of retrieval.

GROUNDED

What the agent knows

Observed, inferred, user-stated or agent-generated — each write declares its epistemic status. An unlabeled write is refused.

VETO
SIMULATED

What the agent imagined

Counterfactuals, hypotheses and plans live here. They cannot be promoted to grounded without explicit confirmation or real-world prediction-error resolution.

Enforced in code. The partition is a storage invariant, not a prompt convention.
Auditable after the fact. Every operation lands in an append-only ledger.

04 · THE EPISTEMIC LABEL

Every fact says how it knows.

Six labels, declared at write time. An unlabeled write is refused — because a defaulted label is indistinguishable from an asserted one at every later read.

OBSERVED

Read directly from a tool result or a source.

INFERRED

Derived by reasoning over observed facts.

USER_STATED

Asserted by the person, not verified by the system.

AGENT_GENERATED

Produced by the agent itself — labeled, never laundered.

HYPOTHESIS

A candidate belief awaiting evidence.

UNLABELED

Rejected at the door by the epistemic veto.

14 · WHAT WE DO NOT CLAIM

The limits, stated plainly.

A launch that hides its gaps gets pulled. These are the four things mem20 does not do today, each with the reason.

Not on PyPI
mem20, mem20-ai and mem20oreo all return 404. Install is source-only, and no doc may claim pip install mem20.
4 of 11 modes unscored
recall, recall_at, entities and ledger have no harness branch. They are named with their blocker rather than filled with estimates.
Not a retrieval leader
The pitch is provenance and auditability, which the repo demonstrates in code. It is not retrieval superiority, which the benchmark does not demonstrate.
Wiring is incomplete
199 of 211 subsystems are not yet reachable by any MCP client. That is criterion 7, and it is the work 4.0 exists to finish.
Every number in this deck is pointable in the repo. A figure that cannot be cited does not ship.
