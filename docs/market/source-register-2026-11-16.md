## Triangulation note (market sizing)

The house TAM of $1.28B is a **slice** of a broad category base. Three independent 2026 bases
for that category are recorded above (`S40` Mordor $6.27B, `S41` SkyQuest $6.16B, `S42` TBRC).
They agree on 2025 to within 5.4% and on growth to within 5 points of CAGR. The house base of
$6.49B sits at the **top** of the published band — a disclosed choice.

**The conditional that must stay attached:** a diligence team using Mordor's $6.27B base and the
same 19.72% share would compute a TAM of about **$1.24B**. That is a hypothetical under a
different base, not a second house figure. Any asset that excerpts it without the conditional
publishes a competing TAM.

The house figures themselves are **carried, never re-derived** — see `docs/finance/inputs.json`
→ `house_numbers.provenance`.
