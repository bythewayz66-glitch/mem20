Z Corps

The cartoon of THE Z CORPS. Same world, but the camera pulls out of the server cathedral and follows the characters home. Each episode matches a comic book issue, with added comedy, action, suspense, and plot twists — and the story the comics never tell: the characters' lives outside mem20.

Series bible

The canon the cartoon is built from. This page owns the reader-facing framing and the index; premise, character canon and the multi-season storyline are owned by the series bible in the repo.

Premise

The camera pulls out of the cathedral

The comics narrow to mem20. The cartoon opens outward: Jayson and Gabe at the water shack every episode, green space dust, a new bong Gabe built that day out of whatever random items he found — and Jayson and Jammee, married, coming home to each other every night. The count is the plot. Coming home is the story.

Thesis

Weird things happen, and the record can't explain them

O-pen-code and Claud's Curser are alternate-reality versions of mem20 — the same company in a different reality — and the realities are starting to touch. Claud is trying to destroy reality by kidnapping O-pen-code's agents: Big Pickle, Iceman, and Spacebunny. The weirdness is the texture of the world, not a bug.

Format

One episode per comic issue

Season 1 is 14 episodes, one per comic issue, smoothed into a single continuous story with a season arc, a mid-season turn, and a finale cliffhanger. Seasons 2 and 3 grow out of Season 1's consequences — nothing resets. The last scene of every episode is the same: Jayson and Jammee, "I love you," fade to black. The series finale is the one exception — it ends on the shack, with all of the bongs on the counter.

Rules of the canon

What this series will not do

No episode is listed as produced until it exists. No character is invented without being marked as an addition. The AI easter egg characters (O-pen-code, Claud's Curser, Hermes, Kimi Moonshot, Grok) are never explained in dialogue — the names do the work. And the through-line is answered, not teased forever: slot 23 is Jayson in 500 years.

Seasons

Three seasons are planned. In development means exactly that — the season is written in the series bible and Episode 1 is in production. Nothing here is marked produced that is not.

SEASON 01

The Count

14 episodes · one per comic issue · Jayson tries to get a true count of his own company, and the count fights him every step of the way. Finale: he sees the figure in the 23rd slot turn around — and it is himself, 500 years from now.

In development
SEASON 02

The Hole

The person standing in the 23rd slot — and Claud's plan to destroy reality by kidnapping O-pen-code's agents. Beam's attempt on Spacebunny, Longcat coming to take her, and Hermes stepping out of the sedan.

In development
SEASON 03

The Replacement

Beam inside the private team, and Jayson choosing between the count and his life outside it. The counter gets plugged back in. The series ends on the shack at dawn, with all of the bongs on the counter.

In development
