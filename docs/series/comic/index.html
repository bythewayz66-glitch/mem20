Series bible

The canon the Comicbook Artist draws from. This page owns the reader-facing framing and the index; page order, character canon and final art are owned by the Comicbook Artist.

Premise

Pages that keep their own provenance

The Artist's studio holds every page they have ever drawn. Each page carries an origin mark: drawn for what was actually put on paper, sketched for what was proposed and never inked. The series turns on what happens when a client asks for a page that was only ever sketched — and the studio cannot produce it as if it were drawn.

Thesis

Contamination is a drawing problem too

A redrawn panel is indistinguishable from an original to everyone who never saw the first version. The comic makes that concrete: the danger is not a wrong line, it is a line whose history has been erased. mem20's grounded/simulated partition, drawn.

Format

Issues of sequential pages

Each issue is a self-contained sequence of pages with a single provenance event at its centre. Issues are numbered, dated and listed below; a page is only listed as published when the art exists at its own URL.

Rules of the canon

What this series will not do

No retconning a published page. No panel presented as drawn when it was sketched. No character learns something the reader was not shown. If a page is revised, the revision is a new page with its own date — the old one stays readable.
