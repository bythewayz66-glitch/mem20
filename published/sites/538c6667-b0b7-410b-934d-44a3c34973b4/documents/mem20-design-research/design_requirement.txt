Requirement - the mem20 desktop user research & design model (design_model.py).

The artifact is the deterministic model behind the mem20 desktop user-research
and design deliverable (Step 08 user research / JTBD, Step 13 experience vision
and wireframes), rebased onto the canonical repository
github.com/bythewayz66-glitch/mem20.

Clauses:
C1. The repository figures must come from run-time sources, not constants: the
    marker document fetched from
    https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bythewayz66-glitch/mem20/main/CANONICAL.md
    supplies the repository identity, and the repository's own Git data API,
    queried with that identity, supplies the tracked-file count and the
    subsystem-package count. The derived figures are then validated against the
    required values: repository bythewayz66-glitch/mem20, default branch main,
    license MIT, 9,882 tracked files, 293 subsystem packages, no package-index
    entry.
C2. The live tool surface must be derived from two independent initialize and
    tools/list handshakes on the canonical stdio server at
    /opt/mem20/mem20/mcp/mcp_server.py. The two handshakes must agree, and the
    derived surface is then validated against the required values: 257 tools,
    protocol 2025-06-18, server name mem20-mcp.
C3. The run exits non-zero instead of emitting a figure when a fetch fails, when a
    handshake fails, when two independent derivations disagree, or when a derived
    figure does not equal its required value.
C4. The model carries four personas (Agent Builder, Platform Engineer, Domain
    Integrator, Memory Operator) and eight desired outcomes, and requires each
    persona's cited evidence tools to be present in the live tool inventory before
    that persona is reported.
C5. The model computes an opportunity score of declared importance plus the
    positive part of declared importance minus declared satisfaction, computes the
    archetype person-count shares from the declared weights so that they sum to 100
    exactly, and validates the required research figures: eight outcomes, four
    personas, five underserved outcomes, underserved mean opportunity 14.4, and
    weighted opportunity index 14.41.
C6. The model evaluates a P0 adoption condition against the rule: underserved mean
    at least 12.0 and at least three underserved outcomes.
C7. The model enforces its invariants with explicit exceptions rather than assert,
    because the interpreter strips assert under -O.
C8. The model supplies its builder with no defaulted source, declares itself as its
    own runnable self-check fixture (gaben_export_a) so a plain run exits zero and
    prints its comparisons, exposes a self-test mode that feeds an altered marker
    and leaves the resulting invariant failure uncaught so the process exits
    non-zero, and contains no placeholder, hardcoded or fabricated repository
    number.
