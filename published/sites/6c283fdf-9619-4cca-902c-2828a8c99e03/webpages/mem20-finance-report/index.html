Reading the two material dependencies

D-02 · Why CAC doubles

The $8,000 blended CAC assumed the OSS-led motion worked: developers find the repository, install from a package index, self-activate, and only the survivor set reaches a sales conversation. Under the canonical repository that assumption has no support — 0 stars, 0 forks, 0 releases, and no PyPI package.

Priced at zero inbound, the same accounts must be reached by outbound and paid channels: $16,800 blended. The $8,000 line is retained in the table as the benchmark to be restored to, not as a current estimate.

D-05 · Why support doubles

Community deflection is not a soft benefit — it was carrying half the support cost. With has_issues=false and has_discussions=false, there is no public channel on which one answered question serves the next ten customers, and no searchable history for self-service.

Every support interaction is therefore a paid, one-to-one interaction: $2,400 per account per year. That single line is what compresses gross margin by 8.6 points.
