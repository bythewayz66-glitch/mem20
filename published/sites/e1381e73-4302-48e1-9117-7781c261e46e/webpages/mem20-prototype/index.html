Method, stated plainly. Tool names, titles and descriptions are read from the repository — 49 of 257. Cluster membership, the persona-affinity weights and the tier split are declared design inputs, not measured preference: they are a hypothesis this usability test is meant to falsify. Rank scores are computed as the share-weighted sum of each persona's opportunity score times that persona's affinity for the cluster, then normalised to 100.
